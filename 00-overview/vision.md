# Product Vision

**Status:** Approved

Nexora is a fictional, portfolio-quality premium Brazilian electronics retailer. It targets Brazil, owns and sells its inventory, and is not a marketplace.

Its catalog includes PC hardware, laptops, smartphones, TVs, monitors, gaming peripherals, networking equipment, smart-home devices, and accessories.

## Ecosystem

- Customer Website
- AI Shopping Assistant
- PC Builder
- Support Center
- Administrative Dashboard
- Shared commerce services

## Product promise

> Make complex technology purchases simple through exceptional search, intelligent recommendations, and a refined shopping experience.
