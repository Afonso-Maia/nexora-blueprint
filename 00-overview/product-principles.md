# Product Principles

**Status:** Approved

## Make complexity understandable

Translate specifications, compatibility constraints, and trade-offs into actionable information.

## Preserve the fast path

Every journey keeps a visible and efficient path to the user’s goal.

## Make guidance optional

Guidance appears at the point of need and remains dismissible.

## Explain recommendations

Recommendations expose reasoning, trade-offs, and confidence.

## Keep users in control

Intent and AI may configure or rank an experience, but users can inspect, modify, or clear their effects.

## Reveal complexity progressively

Show the minimum useful information first and provide technical depth on demand.

## Persist meaningful context

Cart, wishlist, compare list, saved filters, search history, recently viewed products, and PC builds should persist where appropriate.

## Build coherent systems

Search, comparison, and PC Builder form three connected workspaces: Discovery, Decision, and Engineering.
