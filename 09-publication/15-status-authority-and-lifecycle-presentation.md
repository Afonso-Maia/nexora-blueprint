# Status, Authority, and Lifecycle Presentation

**Status:** Approved

## Decision

Show a text status badge and authority statement near every document title. Color may reinforce but never carry status alone. Approved source vocabulary remains intact: Proposed, Approved, Accepted, Planned, In progress, Completed, Superseded, and Deprecated are displayed without publication-specific reinterpretation.

Generated pages identify themselves as “Generated view” or “Orientation” and link to their sources. Controlled implementation values receive a distinct “Controlled selection” treatment that names what remains undecided.

Superseded and deprecated documents remain readable when governance permits, carry a prominent explanation, and link to their successor. Search and navigation retain them with status context rather than presenting them as current.

## Validation

Check visible text, accessible names, contrast, source/metadata agreement, successor links, search-result status, and consistent treatment across themes, print, and narrow screens.
