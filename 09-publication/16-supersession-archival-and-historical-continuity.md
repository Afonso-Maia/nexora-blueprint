# Supersession, Archival, and Historical Continuity

**Status:** Approved

## Decision

Preserve durable canonical URLs for released documentation. Superseded records remain at their canonical route with status, reason where recorded, successor, effective relationship, and access to Git history. Do not rewrite old decisions to appear current.

Use redirects only for route relocation or aliases, not to conceal a superseded source. An archive is a read-only release snapshot or clearly bounded historical collection; the current site remains the default. Exact release-snapshot storage and retention are controlled selections.

Removal requires owner approval, inbound-link analysis, legal/security review where relevant, and a durable replacement or explicit gone response. Git history is not the only reader-facing recovery mechanism.

## Validation

Test redirect chains, canonical tags, successor cycles, archived status visibility, old inbound links, sitemap treatment, search separation, and print/export labeling.
