# Audiences and Reading Modes

**Status:** Approved

## Decision

Serve the complete Blueprint through three complementary reading modes:

1. **Orient** — concise, non-authoritative entry points for first-time readers, portfolio reviewers, and leaders.
2. **Follow** — curated link journeys through a phase, capability, page, template, ADR chain, or delivery outcome.
3. **Reference** — direct access to authoritative documents, identifiers, mappings, and technical detail.

Primary audiences are product, design, engineering, quality, operations and support, governance and leadership, contributors, and portfolio reviewers. The publication does not create audience-specific copies of source truth. Orientation and journeys summarize scope, name their audience and expected time, and link to canonical sources.

No reading mode hides status, authority, prerequisites, controlled values, or consequential caveats. The reference mode remains reachable within one navigation action from every summary.

## Validation

Representative tasks test whether each audience can identify the Blueprint’s purpose, locate an authoritative decision, follow its dependencies, distinguish approved from controlled values, and recover from an incorrect starting point.
