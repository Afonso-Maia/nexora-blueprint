# Delivery Roadmap and Dependency Presentation

**Status:** Approved

## Decision

Present Phase 6 as an outcome-and-dependency model, not a date-based product roadmap. Generate an increment overview for I0–I9, the approved critical commercial spine, prerequisite relationships, planning horizons, gates, exposure stages, and page/template mappings.

Use an accessible dependency list and table as the baseline. A graph is an enhancement with focusable nodes, equivalent text, source links, and bounded detail. Never infer calendar dates, completion percentages, staffing commitments, or provider availability.

Clearly distinguish primary increment assignment from exclusive ownership and distinguish release eligibility from implementation completion. Controlled assumptions remain labelled and link to their registers.

## Validation

Check all ten increments, critical-path order, dependency references, 89-page mapping, nine-template coverage, text/graph equivalence, narrow-screen use, and absence of invented schedule values.
