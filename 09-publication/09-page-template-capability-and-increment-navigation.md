# Page, Template, Capability, and Increment Navigation

**Status:** Approved

## Decision

Generate a connected coverage explorer from the authoritative ledgers for all 89 page IDs and nine templates. A page view links to its surface, archetype/template, source ownership, Design System mapping, engineering profile, testing profile, and primary delivery increment without becoming a new page definition.

Provide complementary indexes by page ID, surface, template, owner/capability, and increment. Counts and relationships are derived mechanically from approved mapping tables. Filters alter presentation only and preserve stable shareable query parameters.

Conflicting mappings, missing IDs, duplicate primary assignments, totals other than 89 pages or nine templates, and links to noncanonical definitions block publication.
