# Theme, Typography, Color, and Brand Adaptation

**Status:** Approved

## Decision

Adapt Nexora’s premium, minimal, engineering-inspired identity through semantic tokens, restrained Precision Gold accents, clear typography, and disciplined spacing. Documentation content uses a highly readable interface/text family with a resilient system fallback; exact font assets remain a controlled implementation selection.

Gold identifies brand and selected emphasis, not warnings, status, links, or authority by itself. Functional semantics retain dedicated tokens. Body measure, type scale, line height, heading rhythm, technical-data treatment, and link distinction prioritize long-form reading.

Publication tokens are a documentation-specific implementation mapped to approved brand principles. They do not become Product Design System tokens or redefine commerce interfaces.

## Validation

Review realistic long documents, headings, tables, code, diagrams, status badges, links, Portuguese diacritics, font failure, zoom, print, and both themes against contrast and readability requirements.
