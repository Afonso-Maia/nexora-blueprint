# Print, Export, and Offline-Reading Boundaries

**Status:** Approved

## Decision

Support high-quality browser printing for individual canonical documents. Print output includes title, status, canonical URL, publication/revision identity when available, expanded essential content, readable links, tables, code, and accessible page-breaking.

Do not initially promise whole-Blueprint PDF, EPUB, installable offline application, or guaranteed offline search. A generated release archive may be introduced later when versioning, size, accessibility, link conversion, and maintenance are proven. Browser save-as-PDF is a convenience, not an authoritative signed edition.

Core document content remains readable when client scripting is unavailable. Cached or downloaded copies visibly lack current-status guarantees and point to the canonical URL.

## Validation

Review representative short, long, tabular, diagram-heavy, and code-heavy documents in print preview; verify theme-independent ink use, link expansion policy, no clipped content, and status/revision visibility.
