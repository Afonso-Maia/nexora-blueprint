# Publication Readiness, Portfolio Journey, and Phase 7 Handoff

**Status:** Approved

## Decision

Use an evidence-led publication gate rather than visual completion. Phase 7 completes only when the repository-native site is implemented, every eligible source is covered, controlled selections required for launch are recorded, representative readers can navigate and understand authority, and operational ownership is ready.

The portfolio journey starts at the product promise, explains the Blueprint and phase sequence, demonstrates one page across product structure, Design System, engineering, testing, and delivery, shows an ADR chain, and ends with publication governance. It remains an optional orientation path with canonical source links.

## Readiness evidence

- all eligible Blueprint documents have one canonical route;
- all 89 pages and nine templates pass cross-phase coverage;
- ADR-0001 through the current ADR are indexed with status and durable links;
- source, metadata, link, anchor, generated-index, and build validation pass;
- WCAG 2.2 AA evidence covers templates, themes, search, navigation, tables, diagrams, and errors;
- responsive, keyboard, assistive-technology, language, print, privacy, security, performance, and resilience gates pass;
- domain, hosting, versions, redirects, indexing, and launch controls are recorded;
- preview, immutable deployment, smoke checks, monitoring, rollback, and recovery are rehearsed;
- contribution, ownership, freshness, dependency, and incident procedures are usable; and
- no publication view changes approved product truth.

## Go/no-go and handoff

Source owners may block for authority or correctness; accessibility and security owners may block within their scope; the Publication Steward blocks for coverage, integrity, or operability. Unresolved blocking evidence prevents public launch.

Completion produces a signed-off evidence index, release identity, known limitations, controlled-value register, owner roster, rollback reference, and post-launch review date. Publication completion does not authorize commerce-product implementation or claim that Nexora has launched commercially.
