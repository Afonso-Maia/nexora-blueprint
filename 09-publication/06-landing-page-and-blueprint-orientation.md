# Landing Page and Blueprint Orientation

**Status:** Approved

## Decision

Use a restrained landing page that answers: what Nexora is, what the Blueprint is, what is authoritative, which phases are complete, how to start, and how to verify a decision. Lead with the product promise and provide direct paths for Explore by phase, Find a decision, Inspect page coverage, Review delivery, and Follow the portfolio journey.

Landing-page prose is orientation, not a replacement for the [Blueprint index](../README.md). Phase status and coverage counts are generated from validated source data. Decorative metrics, fictional commercial results, testimonials, and product-implementation claims are prohibited.

The first viewport prioritizes identity, purpose, authority, and a clear starting action. Brand treatment remains subordinate to reading and traceability.

## Validation

First-time reader testing verifies correct understanding of the fictional product, documentation scope, source authority, completed work, current Phase 7 state, and route to an approved decision.
