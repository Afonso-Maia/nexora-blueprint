# Visual Identity

**Status:** Approved direction

## Precision Gold

Nexora’s visual direction is premium, modern, minimal, engineering-inspired, and professional, with light and dark themes.

It combines Apple’s attention to detail, Stripe’s interface quality, and NVIDIA’s engineering aesthetic while avoiding loud gamer aesthetics, banner-heavy layouts, and visual clutter.

Detailed tokens, typography, iconography, motion, accessibility, components, patterns, and page templates are defined in the completed [Design System and Experience Specification](../04-design-system/README.md).
