# Brand Positioning

**Status:** Approved

Nexora is a premium Brazilian electronics retailer focused on making complex technology purchases simple through exceptional search, intelligent recommendations, and a refined shopping experience.

## Differentiators

- Search-first discovery
- Explainable decision support
- Contextual AI Shopping Copilot
- Compatibility-aware PC Builder
- Premium, low-clutter experience
- First-party catalog and inventory

## Desired perception

Precise, trustworthy, technically competent, modern, and helpful without being intrusive.
