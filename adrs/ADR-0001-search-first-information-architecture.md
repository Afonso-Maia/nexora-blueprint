# ADR-0001: Search-First Information Architecture

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-02
- **Context:** Reconstructed record of a previously approved decision

## Context

Nexora needs a premium discovery architecture that supports a growing technical catalog, multiple decision-support content types, contextual AI, and PC configuration without creating dense navigation.

## Decision

- Adopt a search-first global information architecture.
- Make Universal Search the primary interaction.
- Rank products first for commercial queries.
- Keep categories accessible through a single mega menu.
- Keep Deals, PC Builder, and Support as primary tasks.
- Treat AI, Account, Wishlist, Compare, and Cart as global utilities.
- Make the homepage a discovery hub.
- Let merchandising support but not dominate discovery.

## Benefits

- Search, AI, and PC Builder become coherent first-class experiences.
- Navigation remains compact as the catalog grows.
- Multiple content types support product decisions.
- The architecture aligns with premium positioning.

## Costs and risks

- Search quality becomes business-critical.
- Category browsing must remain accessible.
- Ranking and content governance require ongoing ownership.

## Consequences

New features must conform to the governance in [Discovery Architecture Validation](../02-information-architecture/07-validation.md). Search behavior and content ownership must be explicit.
