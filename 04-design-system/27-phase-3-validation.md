## Architectural invariants

Validation confirms that the Design System preserves:

- Search-first interaction priority
- Hybrid navigation with one Categories mega menu
- Governed taxonomy and attribute-driven filters
- Visible, modifiable, and clearable intent
- Optional, labelled, and explainable AI
- Deterministic Compatibility authority
- Progressive disclosure without hidden consequential facts
- Meaningful state persistence
- Direct customer fast paths
- One Comparison Decision Workspace
- One PC Builder Engineering Workspace
- One unified Checkout
- Federated Account continuity
- Shared typed Support cases
- Stable capability-based Admin navigation
- Permission-aware presentation and source enforcement
- Host-owned operation-aware states
- Responsive semantic continuity
- Light and dark theme parity
- Accessibility as a blocking foundation

No Phase 3 specification changes page ownership, source-domain lifecycle, product taxonomy, permission semantics, Compatibility facts, or operational authority.

## Layer validation

The six-layer model remains coherent:

- Foundations define system-wide intent and constraints.
- Tokens encode reusable named decisions.
- Primitives provide low-level semantic behavior.
- Components compose reusable interface units.
- Patterns coordinate product tasks and state transitions.
- Page templates realize approved archetypes.

The separate-asset threshold prevents cosmetic component proliferation. Domain extensions remain above the primitive layer and consume shared foundations.

## Theme validation

Validation confirms:

- Stable semantic roles across light and dark
- Complete surface, content, border, action, selection, focus, and functional-state mappings
- Independent informational, positive, cautionary, and critical semantics
- Gold remains brand and emphasis rather than status
- Product imagery is not recolored by theme
- Dark depth uses surfaces and borders rather than shadow alone
- Theme selection preserves task state
- Representative text, focus, action, and status pairs meet declared contrast thresholds

Final component contrast remains subject to actual adjacent colors and rendering.

## Typography and content validation

Validation confirms:

- One variable primary family and governed fallback
- Semantic display, heading, body, label, data, and caption roles
- Tabular numeric behavior for comparison and operational data
- Monospace limited to identifiers and code-like values
- Brazilian Portuguese diacritics, grammar, formatting, and expansion
- Real, installment, date, unit, address, and technical-data behavior
- Truncation prohibitions for critical facts
- Font and content failure preserve task access

## Responsive and density validation

Validation confirms:

- Content-driven thresholds rather than device assumptions
- One semantic system from constrained through data-wide
- Primary goals and constraints remain visible
- Drawers and tabs preserve active state
- Sticky regions do not cover focus or recovery
- Comfortable is the default
- Compact increases information density without reducing target safety, labels, permission, or capability
- Guarded larger-workspace continuation is based on task risk and density

## Accessibility validation

The approved baseline is WCAG 2.2 AA across:

- All pages and templates
- Both themes
- Both density modes
- Responsive compositions
- Host-owned states
- Keyboard, touch, pointer, voice, and assistive technology
- Zoom, reflow, forced color, text scaling, and reduced motion

Enhanced internal requirements cover effective `44 × 44px` default targets, strong focus appearance, authentication, critical commerce, and high-consequence Admin work.

Accessibility stewardship has blocking authority. Component approval does not substitute for page- and journey-level validation.

## Tier 1 journey validation

### Known-item Search to Purchase

Search, results, Product Detail, Cart, Checkout, confirmation, and Order Detail share product facts, visible state, direct actions, and safe recovery.

### Browse Categories to Purchase

Guided and Expert intent share one result grid, filter state, Product Detail, and purchase path.

### Product Comparison to Purchase

Comparison layers decisive differences, evidence, Compatibility, explainable recommendation, and direct validated purchase.

### AI Recommendation to Purchase

AI remains optional, labelled, evidence-aware, editable, and unable to override facts or act without explicit control.

### PC Builder to Purchase

Guided and Expert initialization enter one persistent workspace; Compatibility, build state, Cart grouping, and Checkout continuity remain intact.

### Cart to Checkout

Cart corrects invalid lines; one adaptive Checkout preserves inputs, review, exact totals, idempotent commitment, and authoritative outcome.

## Residual downstream inputs

Phase 3 does not approve:

- Implementation framework, packages, APIs, storage, or deployment
- Exact font delivery, code-level token format, or component API
- Final high-fidelity wireframes and production visual compositions
- Production photography, illustrations, icons, and campaign artwork
- Final legal text and jurisdiction-specific disclosures
- Exact authentication methods, risk thresholds, and Security policy
- Exact financial, operational, approval, capacity, and performance thresholds
- Analytics events and observability implementation

These inputs may refine implementation and configuration but cannot silently change approved semantics, hierarchy, state, accessibility, ownership, or authority.
