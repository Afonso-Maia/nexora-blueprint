No test or metric becomes source truth. No legal, policy, provider, capacity, browser-version, staffing, or schedule value reserved to a controlled register or later phase is silently approved.

## Delivery Roadmap handoff

The Delivery Roadmap may now sequence:

- implementation slices and dependencies;
- staffing and specialist capacity;
- tool procurement and governed technical selections;
- environment and automation delivery;
- milestones, release trains, launch waves, rollout, and dates;
- operational readiness work and provider onboarding; and
- incremental satisfaction of the approved quality gates.

It must consume Phase 5 evidence requirements. It cannot weaken a gate, convert missing evidence into schedule acceptance, change source authority, or treat the complete strategy as a requirement to automate every combination before the first safe increment.

## Controlled implementation registers

Implementation maintains versioned registers for exact tools and versions, browser/device/AT support, provider sandbox/live modes, environment topology, data revisions, workload/capacity assumptions, evidence retention, scheduled drill cadence, thresholds, and qualified authorities. Selections follow existing governance and cannot alter the strategy.

## Handoff result

Nexora is ready for Delivery Roadmap planning. Production implementation remains outside this documentation phase until explicitly authorized.
