# Product Decisions

Product decisions are recorded in their relevant topic files using the statuses Proposed, Approved, Superseded, or Deprecated. Cross-cutting or expensive-to-reverse decisions also receive an [ADR](../adrs/README.md).

Phase 1, Phase 2A, Phase 2B, and Phase 3 decisions are approved and complete. Phase 4 decisions are in progress and indexed in [Engineering Architecture and Implementation Planning](../06-engineering/README.md). Product Structure decisions are indexed in [Product Structure Architecture](../03-product-structure/README.md), and Phase 3 decisions are indexed in [Design System and Experience Specification](../04-design-system/README.md).
