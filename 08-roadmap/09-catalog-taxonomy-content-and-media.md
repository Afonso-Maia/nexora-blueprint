# Catalog, Taxonomy, Content, and Media Foundation

**Status:** Approved

## Decision

Deliver Catalog as governed source truth before Search, Compatibility, merchandising, or AI projections.

I0 proves stable product/variant identity, module schema, versioned governed attributes, revision lifecycle, ingestion quarantine, provenance, media contracts, and rebuildable projection events with representative synthetic products.

I1 adds controlled ingestion, validation workflows, published revisions, category and brand content, product media, buying guides, policy-aware content, projection reconciliation, and the first production-readiness data set. Admin catalog and content workspaces ship with the source operations they govern.

Compatibility facts are added under joint Catalog/Compatibility governance before I2. Pricing and Inventory remain separate authorities and are composed rather than copied into Catalog truth.

## Gates

No product becomes discoverable until its required attributes, localization, media accessibility, provenance, publication status, and projection evidence pass. Bulk imports are resumable, quarantined, auditable, and reversible through governed revisions.
