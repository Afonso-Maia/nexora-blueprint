# Milestones, Checkpoints, and Decision Gates

**Status:** Approved

## Decision

Express milestones as evidence states, never percentages complete.

Every increment has:

1. **Frame:** approved outcome, scope, pages/templates, risk, owner, assumptions.
2. **Ready:** dependencies, capacity, provider, data, environment, acceptance, and test seams credible.
3. **Integrated:** authoritative outcome works in a faithful environment with diagnostics.
4. **Candidate:** Done evidence and dossier assembled against the immutable artifact.
5. **Expose:** stage-specific authorities approve bounded exposure and halt conditions.
6. **Learn/close:** telemetry, reconciliation, support findings, debt, and follow-up decisions recorded.

Cross-program checkpoints cover provider selection, production environment, data rehearsal, security/privacy, accessibility, performance/capacity, recovery, operational/support readiness, pilot, beta, and GA.

## Governance

The accountable owner declares evidence; assigned authorities accept their portions; Delivery confirms dependency and capacity state. A gate can pass, fail, or return with actions. Conditional passage requires an allowed bounded residual risk; Q0 gaps cannot pass conditionally.
