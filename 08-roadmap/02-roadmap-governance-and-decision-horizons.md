# Roadmap Governance and Decision Horizons

**Status:** Approved

## Decision

Use rolling, evidence-led planning rather than a fixed end-to-end delivery calendar.

- **Committed:** at most two increments with Ready evidence, accountable owners, capacity, satisfied entry dependencies, and credible exit evidence.
- **Forecast:** dependency-ordered work with ranges and explicit confidence, not promised dates.
- **Directional:** complete capability and page coverage without delivery-date precision.

The Delivery Lead maintains the roadmap; Product owns outcome priority; source and experience owners own feasibility; Platform, Quality, Security, Privacy, Accessibility, and Operations retain their approved blocking authority.

## Movement and review

Committed work is reviewed weekly at delivery control and at every material assumption change. Forecast and directional horizons are reviewed at increment gates and at least monthly during active delivery.

Promotion requires Ready, dependency, staffing, provider, environment, data, and specialist evidence. Demotion or replanning occurs when an assumption fails, a gate blocks, scope changes materially, or urgent risk consumes capacity. No milestone is protected by weakening scope authority or quality evidence.

Roadmap records distinguish target, forecast, and commitment. A calendar date is never a commitment without its scope, confidence, assumptions, and decision owner.

## Outputs

Each review records horizon movements, changed assumptions, evidence, owner, consequences, and the next decision date in the linked roadmap registers.
