# Phase 6 — Delivery Roadmap and Incremental Release Planning

**Status:** In progress

Phase 6 defines a practical, dependency-aware implementation and release roadmap for a small initial organization. It sequences usable increments, enabling runway, staffing and specialist capacity, environments, providers, milestones, release trains, launch waves, and operational readiness without redefining the approved Product Blueprint.

## Approved decisions

1. [Framing and Delivery-Planning Model](01-framing-and-delivery-planning-model.md) — approved; risk-led vertical increments with bounded enabling runway, three planning horizons, controlled-value boundaries, and integrated completion criteria

## Phase boundary

Phase 6 consumes the approved product, Design System, engineering, and quality contracts. It may decide sequencing, dependencies, staffing assumptions, procurement timing, environments, milestones, exposure stages, and readiness work.

It does not produce production implementation code, weaken release evidence, redesign canonical pages or source authority, invent legal or operational policy, or substitute schedule pressure for an approved architecture or quality outcome.
