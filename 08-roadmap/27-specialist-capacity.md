# Specialist-Capacity Planning

**Status:** Approved

## Decision

Book specialist capacity by risk and gate, and invest in reusable enablement so specialists do not become late review queues.

Required capability registers cover Accessibility, Security, Privacy, Quality Engineering, SRE/operations, data, content/localization, Brazilian legal/commerce policy, procurement, and provider expertise.

Each forecast increment identifies consultation, design review, evidence production, independent approval, drill, and launch-support needs. Q0/Q1 work reserves capacity before commitment. Scarce external assessment lead times—penetration testing, disabled-user research, legal review, recovery exercises, or provider certification—enter the dependency graph early.

Specialists publish patterns, threat/risk models, checklists, test harnesses, office hours, and delegation boundaries. Feature owners remain responsible for remediation.

## Gate

Missing blocking authority or qualified evidence demotes the increment or reduces exposure; it is not replaced by delivery-lead acceptance. Exact named personnel and schedules remain controlled staffing values.
