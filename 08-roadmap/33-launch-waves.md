# Launch-Wave and Capability Rollout Strategy

**Status:** Approved

## Decision

Launch progressively around a safe commerce spine while preserving deterministic alternatives and explicit capability scope.

| Wave | Intended capability |
| --- | --- |
| W0 — Workforce/internal | delivery controls, governed data, operational and Admin source capabilities |
| W1 — Discovery preview | public content, Catalog, Search, filtering, product evaluation without consequential purchase |
| W2 — Commerce pilot | controlled products/cohorts, Cart, unified Checkout, payment, Order, confirmation, support coverage |
| W3 — Continuity beta | broader commerce plus fulfillment, Account, notifications, and typed Support Cases |
| W4 — Guided expansion | PC Builder and optional AI after their independent gates; broader catalog/providers |
| W5 — GA | declared supported scope with sustainable operations and complete evidence |

Admin capability slices precede or accompany the customer operations they support. Legal/informational and recovery destinations required by a wave ship before that wave.

## Control

Exact cohorts, products, payment/delivery methods, traffic, dates, and geography remain controlled launch values. Wave reduction is a safe scope/exposure decision only when it remains coherent, accessible, supportable, and does not remove required authority or recovery.
