# Scope Change and Roadmap Replanning

**Status:** Approved

## Decision

Use impact-led rolling replanning. Baselines provide accountability but are not frozen plans.

A change request identifies outcome, reason, affected pages/templates/domains, Q/C risk, dependencies, capacity, provider, data, evidence, operations, debt, exposure, and opportunity cost.

Local reversible changes within an approved increment follow normal ownership. Changes affecting committed outcome, critical path, cross-domain contract, provider, specialist gate, launch scope, or date confidence require roadmap review. Changes to approved product truth, authority, permission, lifecycle, accessibility, security, privacy, or architecture return to Blueprint governance.

## Response options

Accept with updated evidence; swap equal-priority scope; split into coherent increments; move to forecast/directional; reduce exposure while preserving a valid slice; add capacity where the constraint is parallelizable; or reject.

Quality gates, recovery, authority, and operational readiness are not negotiable scope buffers.

## Record

Replanning updates horizons, assumptions, dependencies, risks, milestones, page mapping, exposure, and stakeholder communication in one decision record.
