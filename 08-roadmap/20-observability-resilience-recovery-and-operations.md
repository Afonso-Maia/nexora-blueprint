# Observability, Resilience, Recovery, and Operational Tooling

**Status:** Approved

## Decision

Grow operational capability along the delivery spine, using source outcomes rather than infrastructure health alone.

I0 establishes OpenTelemetry conventions, release/configuration correlation, privacy controls, basic service objectives as controlled assumptions, alert ownership, runbook templates, backup automation, and restore harnesses.

I1 adds Search/Catalog projection lag and rebuild diagnostics. I2 adds Compatibility and commercial freshness/reconciliation. I3 adds Checkout operation, payment ambiguity, queue, idempotency, and at-most-one-Order diagnostics. I4–I5 add provider delivery and case-obligation operations. I6–I8 add workspace, AI, and workforce-specific controls.

## Readiness

Each increment defines golden signals, business/source outcomes, halt conditions, degradation, reconciliation, capacity, backup/restore, and incident procedures. Drills advance from component failure through dependency outage, data restore, provider ambiguity, and recoverable regional rebuild according to exposure risk.

No dashboard without an owner and action is release evidence. Exact thresholds, recovery targets, and drill cadence remain controlled and evidence-calibrated.
