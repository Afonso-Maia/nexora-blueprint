# Success Measures and Post-Release Learning Loops

**Status:** Approved

## Decision

Measure whether each increment produces its approved outcome safely and sustainably. Do not collapse success into revenue, delivery velocity, test pass rate, or one health score.

Every increment defines:

- outcome measures tied to the user or workforce job;
- guardrails for authority, accessibility, security, privacy, support, and harmful failure;
- experience measures for discovery, comprehension, completion, recovery, and performance;
- source/operational measures for correctness, ambiguity, reconciliation, backlog, freshness, capacity, and incidents;
- adoption and exposure measures interpreted against cohort and availability; and
- learning questions, decision thresholds or judgment method, owner, cadence, and response.

Measures observe but do not become source truth. Exact baselines and targets are controlled values calibrated from representative evidence.

## Learning cadence

Internal and pilot stages review daily or per exposure event as risk requires; active beta reviews at least weekly; stable GA moves to an owned operating cadence. Incidents, support themes, accessibility findings, reconciliation, provider drift, and abandoned journeys feed the same review.

The response may advance, hold, reduce, or stop exposure; repair a defect; change operations; retire debt; recalibrate an assumption; or propose a governed Blueprint change. Learning does not silently redefine approved behavior.
