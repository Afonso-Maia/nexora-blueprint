# Release Evidence and Go/No-Go Governance

**Status:** Approved

## Decision

Make exposure decisions from the approved immutable-artifact dossier and stage scope.

The Release owner assembles artifact/source/configuration/migration/contract/ruleset identities; affected pages/domains/risks; gate results; manual specialist evidence; provider/environment/data provenance; defects/quarantines/exceptions; operational controls; last known-good artifact; repair/reconciliation posture; exposure cohort; telemetry and halt conditions.

Source and experience owners accept behavior; Product accepts intended outcome and scope; Security, Privacy, Accessibility, Quality, and Operations exercise assigned blocking authority; Delivery confirms dependency/capacity and may approve exposure only for an **Eligible** or policy-permitted **Conditionally eligible** candidate.

Q0 gaps, absent authority, unsafe deployment/recovery, or missing evidence are **Not eligible**. Schedule, sunk cost, or executive preference does not convert eligibility.

## Decision record

Every go/no-go records stage, scope, artifact, evidence, approvers, dissent/blockers, residual risks, conditions, exposure plan, halt authority, and next review. Automated health may halt progression; humans retain authority to reduce or stop exposure.
