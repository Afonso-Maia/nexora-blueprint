# Documentation, Training, and Workforce Enablement

**Status:** Approved

## Decision

Deliver documentation and competency with each capability. Publication styling remains a later phase, but operational truth cannot wait for the documentation site.

Every increment updates contracts, architecture and decision records, data/event/API references, configuration, provider procedures, test/evidence ownership, dashboards, alerts, runbooks, recovery, known limitations, support content, and change notes as applicable.

Admin and support capabilities require role-based training using synthetic scenarios, accessibility-aware materials, restricted-field and privacy instruction, approval/assurance procedures, bulk/export safety, incident escalation, and demonstrated task competence before production access.

Operations exercises runbooks through tabletop and technical drills. Provider contacts, console steps, evidence collection, reconciliation, and exit procedures are practiced rather than merely stored.

## Gate

Documentation has an owner, effective version, audience, review trigger, and discoverable location. Missing runbooks, untrained operators, or unverified support procedures block the corresponding exposure.
