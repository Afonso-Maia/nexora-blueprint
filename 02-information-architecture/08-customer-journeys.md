# Tier 1 Customer Journeys

**Status:** Approved

## J-01 — Known-item Search to Purchase

**Model:** Adaptive Journey

`Home → Universal Search → Results → Product → Cart → Checkout`

Filters, compatibility checks, accessories, comparison, and AI guidance remain contextual and optional. The direct purchase path stays visible.

## J-02 — Browse Categories to Purchase

**Model:** Intent-Driven Discovery

Goals may include gaming, work, study, programming, video editing, travel, and everyday use. Intent may configure ranking and filtering, but never replaces the catalog; effects remain visible, modifiable, and clearable. Guided and expert paths converge on the same grid.

## J-03 — Product Comparison to Purchase

**Model:** Decision Workspace

Layer Executive Summary, Key Differences, Use-Case Recommendations, Compatibility, Price and Value Context, Full Technical Comparison, and optional AI Summary. Recommendations disclose confidence, rationale, trade-offs, and conditions favoring another choice. Selection preserves context.

> Comparison should reduce cognitive load, not increase it.

## J-04 — AI Recommendation to Purchase

**Model:** AI Shopping Copilot

AI is contextual across search, categories, comparison, product detail, PC Builder, and checkout; a dedicated page supports open-ended conversations. AI is optional, explainable, non-intrusive, user-controlled, and separate from deterministic compatibility facts.

## J-05 — PC Builder to Purchase

**Model:** Engineering Workspace

Guided and Expert initialization lead to one non-linear workspace. See [PC Builder Architecture](09-pc-builder.md).

## J-06 — Cart to Checkout

**Model:** Adaptive Checkout

One unified page contains modular Contact, Delivery, Payment, Review, Promotions, Financing, Warranty, and Place Order sections. It supports guests, returning customers, progressive disclosure, applicable financing and warranties, PC Build summaries, and optional AI assistance.
