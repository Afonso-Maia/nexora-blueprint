# Discovery Architecture Validation

**Status:** Approved

Phase 2A was validated across navigation and sitemap, navigation and search, search and taxonomy, taxonomy and collections, filters and taxonomy, shared compatibility, AI boundaries, and growth from thousands to 100,000+ products.

## Discovery priority

1. Search
2. Categories
3. Recommendations and Collections
4. AI Assistant
5. Editorial content

This determines emphasis, not availability.

## Content ownership

- Catalog owns products and governed attributes.
- Marketing owns collections and promotions.
- Support owns help content.
- AI consumes authoritative content but does not own it.

## Feature lifecycle governance

Every new feature declares its navigation entry, search behavior, taxonomy impact, filter impact, URL strategy, permissions, mobile behavior, and owning domain.
